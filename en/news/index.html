World top 5 stories most widely covered, with a focus on the Middle East

  1. 1
    Saudi-led coalition strikes Yemen's Hodeidah port; Houthis warn they may target Jeddah
    • The Saudi-led coalition struck Hodeidah port in Yemen on the 20th. It first told civilians to leave, and said it destroyed facilities used to bring in weapons and missiles. Hodeidah is the main port for food and supplies going into northern Yemen.
    • The Houthis (an Iran-aligned armed group in Yemen) warned that Jeddah and other Saudi Red Sea ports could be targeted, and told airlines that Saudi airspace is "unsafe" (except over Mecca and Medina).
    • At his news conference on the 20th, President Trump accused Iran of not negotiating in good faith and said he wants a "real deal," not a fake one.
    Why it matters: this follows the attack on a Riyadh tower on the 19th, and the fighting could spread to the Red Sea side. Oil and shipping are affected.
    Sources: The National / Al-Monitor / Gulf News / The New Arab / NBC News / CBS News
  2. 2
    Russia launches a massive attack on Ukraine, hitting three Kyiv bridges and power facilities
    • Ukraine's Air Force says Russia launched 84 cruise missiles and 404 drones. President Zelensky says 27 ballistic missiles were also used.
    • According to the Kyiv Independent, at least nine people were killed, three bridges in Kyiv were damaged, and electricity, heating and water were cut in some areas. Europe's largest substation was also targeted.
    • The day before, the leaders of Germany, France, the UK and Ukraine met in Berlin and called for a halt to attacks on energy facilities over the winter. President Putin reportedly said he would not negotiate.
    Why it matters: the attack came right after the US–China–Russia "peace declaration," and it directly threatens people's lives as winter begins.
    Sources: Kyiv Independent / Kyiv Independent (substation) / Kyiv Independent (Berlin) / Al Jazeera / Kyiv Post (ISW) / CNN
  3. 3
    Stocks fall sharply around the world: Nikkei drops 3,007 yen, US Nasdaq tumbles
    • Japan's Nikkei fell 3,007 yen (about 5%) on the 20th, its fifth-largest fall ever in yen terms. Australian and Chinese shares also fell.
    • In the US, the Dow lost about 1,100 points on the 19th, and on the afternoon of the 20th the Nasdaq was down more than 4% (closing figures not yet confirmed).
    • AI and chip stocks are being sold. Anthropic's listing did not calm worries, and fears of higher US interest rates added to the pressure.
    Why it matters: shares that rose on hopes for AI are falling at the same time around the world. Pensions and investment funds are affected too.
    Sources: Yahoo Finance / The Globe and Mail / CBS News / Kabutan / ABC News
  4. 4
    Back home, Trump defends his relationship with Putin; no deal on Ukraine
    • After returning from Asia, President Trump held a news conference at the White House on the 20th. He said the three-way talks with Xi and Putin were centred on Ukraine.
    • However, the leaders' joint declaration does not mention Ukraine or any new peace effort. Asked about Russia's massive attack right after the meeting, he defended his relationship with President Putin.
    • Between the US and China, issues such as trade, Taiwan and chips are reported to remain unresolved.
    Sources: CBS News / NBC News / CNN / CNBC / PBS / Fox News
  5. 5
    Gaza: local anti-Hamas armed groups accused of taking part in Israel's operationLittle covered in Japan
    • According to Al Jazeera, a Palestinian security source said local armed groups opposed to Hamas took part in the Israeli army's operation in central Gaza.
    • Gaza's Government Media Office says about 6,000 people were forced from their homes by the operation. Those who returned found their homes in ruins.
    • Gaza's Civil Defence is reported to be struggling to rescue people because its vehicles keep breaking down.
    Why it matters: in a Gaza that is supposed to be under a ceasefire, both daily life and the means of helping people are getting weaker.
    Sources: Al Jazeera / Al Jazeera (video) / Al Jazeera (Middle East) / Al Jazeera (Nov 18)
