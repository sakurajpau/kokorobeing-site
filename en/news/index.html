World top 5 with a focus on the Middle East

  1. 1
    US and Iran: on the edge of renewed war
    • A third US aircraft carrier, the USS Theodore Roosevelt, has arrived in the Middle East. The naval blockade of Iranian ports continues; about 150 ships have been turned away since June.
    • On Sunday Iran launched six drones at a US air base in Bahrain. Bahrain says it intercepted several of them.
    • Through a Qatari mediator, Iran has sent seven conditions, including lifting the blockade and sanctions. Trump says the US does not need a deal.
    Why it matters: much of the world's oil, including most of Japan's, passes through the Strait of Hormuz. Oil prices react directly.
    Sources: Al Jazeera / PBS (AP) / The Hill / CBS News
  2. 2
    Yemen: Houthi drones aimed at Mecca and RiyadhLittle covered in Japan
    • The Saudi-led coalition says the Houthis launched two drones toward Mecca and Riyadh.
    • The Houthis say Saudi air strikes killed three people, including a child. Saudi Arabia has not commented.
    • Houthi forces are reported to be moving toward Aden. Saudi Arabia's Jizan refinery has been hit three times.
    Sources: Khaleej Times / PBS (AP) / Foreign Policy
  3. 3
    Gaza: a ceasefire in name, a hard winter ahead
    • Gaza health authorities say more than 1,600 people have been killed since the ceasefire began.
    • The Red Cross says 1.5 million people urgently need shelter before winter. Tents, caravans and fuel are short.
    • Australia, Canada, Italy and the UK have called on Israel to let aid in without obstruction.
    Sources: Gulf News / British Red Cross / UNN / CNN
  4. 4
    Lebanon: Israel's first deep strike in monthsLittle covered in Japan
    • Israel says it hit a weapons shipment bound for Hezbollah in northeastern Lebanon, near the Syrian border.
    • A US envoy says Hezbollah must disarm.
    Source: PBS (AP)
  5. 5
    Ukraine: the largest drone attack on Russia of the war
    • Ukraine struck a key bridge and oil refineries. Zelensky says over half of Russia's refining capacity is out; industry sources put it at about 30%.
    • A Russian strike on the train station in Dnipro killed 15 people.
    • Peace talks produced agreement on the largest prisoner exchange of the war. European countries will formally join the next round for the first time.
    Sources: NBC News (Reuters) / CNN / Washington Post / Kyiv Post
