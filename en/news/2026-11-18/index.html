World top 5 with a focus on the Middle East

  1. 1
    US strikes Iran's Qeshm Island; the Strait of Hormuz stays almost shut
    • A US military official says US forces struck launchers on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, in response to the Revolutionary Guard targeting US personnel.
    • Iran again sent drones toward Bahrain; US Central Command says they were intercepted with no damage.
    • Iran says it will not reopen the strait or return to talks until the blockade and sanctions are lifted. Al Jazeera asks whether this is becoming a "frozen conflict".
    Why it matters: almost no ships are passing the strait, which directly affects world oil and Japan's energy.
    Sources: CBS News / ABC News / Al Jazeera
  2. 2
    Yemen: Houthis take the southern coast near the mouth of the Red SeaLittle covered in Japan
    • Saudi-backed government forces pulled back to defend Aden and withdrew from positions near the Bab al-Mandab Strait.
    • Saudi Arabia replaced the commander of the coalition backing Yemen's government.
    • A Houthi missile attack near Aden reportedly killed eight people. The Houthis say Saudi strikes killed three, including a child.
    Why it matters: the mouth of the Red Sea is a key shipping lane between Asia and Europe.
    Sources: The New Arab / The New Arab / PBS (AP)
  3. 3
    Gaza and Lebanon: attacks continue despite the ceasefire
    • Gaza health authorities say more than 1,600 people have been killed since the ceasefire began. Two people, including a six-year-old boy, were reportedly killed in Deir el-Balah.
    • Israel says it struck a weapons shipment for Hezbollah in northeastern Lebanon, its first strike that deep in months.
    Sources: The New Arab / PBS (AP)
  4. 4
    Ukraine partly destroys the Crimea bridge; Kyiv bridges hit too
    • Ukraine's security service says one steel arch and the road span of the Kerch Bridge were destroyed and the rail section damaged. Russia has opened a "terrorism" case.
    • Russia's defence ministry says it downed 1,441 drones in a second night of large attacks.
    • Russian strikes damaged and closed two main bridges in Kyiv, killing two, and hit power facilities in eight regions.
    Sources: NPR / Al Jazeera / NBC News
  5. 5
    Trump lands in Manila; US–China–Russia summit set for Thursday
    • After ASEAN, Trump heads to APEC in Shenzhen for the first-ever three-way meeting with Xi and Putin.
    • New US rules tighten exports of advanced AI chips. China's foreign ministry objected and Asian stocks fell.
    Sources: CNN / Fox News / FXStreet
