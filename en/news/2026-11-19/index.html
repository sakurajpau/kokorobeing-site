World top 5 stories most widely covered, with a focus on the Middle East

  1. 1
    US and Iran trade strikes; US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain targeted
    • US forces struck a Revolutionary Guard missile launch site on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, calling it self-defence.
    • Iran then sent drones at Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait, which hosts US forces, and at Bahrain. Both countries say they intercepted them. It is Iran's first attack on Kuwait in two months.
    • Iran's navy says three oil tankers hit mines east of the strait. Qatar warned of "grave danger" in the Gulf and called for talks with Iran.
    Why it matters: a war that seemed to be freezing is spreading again, with direct effects on oil and Japan's energy.
    Sources: Al Jazeera / The New Arab / CNN / TIME / CBS News / Anadolu
  2. 2
    Gaza: Israel's first ground operation since the ceasefire, in central Gaza
    • Israeli forces moved beyond the ceasefire's Yellow Line near the Nuseirat refugee camp; tanks reportedly took parts of the main north–south road.
    • Al Jazeera reports three Palestinians killed and many fleeing tents and shelters. Israel says it is removing a threat to its troops.
    • The WHO chief called for unobstructed aid as Gaza faces its fourth winter of war.
    Sources: Al Jazeera / France 24 / CP24 / Times of Israel / Anadolu
  3. 3
    APEC opens in Shenzhen; Xi greets Trump in person
    • Xi Jinping personally welcomed Donald Trump. A first-ever US–China–Russia leaders' meeting is planned.
    • Xi pledged to oppose protectionism; Putin criticised Western sanctions.
    • Earlier, Quad leaders issued a statement voicing serious concern about "economic coercion".
    Sources: NHK / CNN / Fox News / Anadolu
  4. 4
    Nvidia beats forecasts, but its shares can't hold the gain
    • Revenue was about $115.8 billion, more than double a year earlier; next-quarter guidance is about $130 billion.
    • Shares gave up an early rise and swung up and down, with worries about life after Jensen Huang.
    • US stocks fell and the dollar hit a 19-month high.
    Sources: Investing.com / Yahoo Finance / NBC News / Benzinga
  5. 5
    Yemen: Houthis push toward Aden; Saudi Arabia answers with heavy air strikesLittle covered in Japan
    • Houthi forces are advancing along the southern coast toward Aden, and Saudi Arabia has responded with large-scale air strikes.
    • The Houthis say Saudi strikes killed three people, including a child.
    Why it matters: the fighting is close to the mouth of the Red Sea, a key shipping lane between Asia and Europe.
    Sources: The New Arab / The New Arab / PBS (AP)
